Content Report

Review ·Version: v1 · Author: YouCam Muse · Date: 2026-10-29 ·Guideline: Open ↗

Report a song or music video with a built-in reason or an explanation. Reviewers can remove public access while the creator keeps private playback.

Reading this spec. This is a behavior specification for the front-end RD, not production code — the prototype source cannot be reused. Each step card shows the screenshot first, then what the user does (bold) and what the WEB UI shows, with on-screen text, rules & limits, and input/output below. On a screenshot, a solid red frame marks the click that advances the flow; a dashed amber frame marks a key point to note (hover a frame for its label). Steps carry a stable ID (e.g. P1-S2).

P1Choose a reason and submit

Visitors and signed-in users follow the same flow for other people's and official content.

  1. P1-S1 Presses Report on a music video.
  2. P1-S2 Selects one built-in reason.
  3. P1-S3 Selects Something else and enters Details.
  4. P1-S4 Presses Submit Report.

Rules
  • No sign-in is required.

    P2Duplicate and session limit

    Exactly two controls prevent repeated or excessive reports in one document lifetime.

    1. P2-S1 Presses Report on content already successfully reported in this session.
    2. P2-S2 Successfully reports five items, then presses Report on a sixth different item.
    P2-S1
    TRIGGERPresses Report on content already successfully reported in this session.
    WEB UIA neutral toast appears and the report dialog stays closed.
    Strings
    • You already reported this content.
    Rules
    • Duplicate feedback wins even when the five-report cap is also reached.
    • Song and MV keys are distinct even when their IDs match.
    P2-S2
    TRIGGERSuccessfully reports five items, then presses Report on a sixth different item.
    WEB UIAn amber toast appears and the report dialog stays closed.
    Strings
    • You have reached the report limit for this session.
    Rules
    • MV and Song share five successful submissions; the fifth is allowed.
    • Cancelled and failed attempts do not count.
    • Client navigation and sign-in changes preserve the session; refresh or a new document resets it.
    • Both guard toasts last three seconds and hold on hover or focus; leaving restarts three seconds.
    • Phone toasts sit above the tab bar.
    P3Failure and retry

    The user can recover without losing the report draft.

    1. P3-S1 Submits while the submission service fails.
    2. P3-S2 Presses Submit Report again after the failure is resolved.

      P4Own content is not reportable

      Official content remains reportable; ownership is based on the signed-in account.

      1. P4-S1 Opens their own song result while signed in.
      P4-S1
      TRIGGEROpens their own song result while signed in.
      WEB UIThe Report entry is absent.
      Strings
        Rules
        • Reject attempted self-report submissions as well as hiding the entry.
        • Visitors can report public content; after sign-in evaluate ownership against the account.
        • The reported creator is not shown the reporter's identity.
        • Reporter identification or logging is defined by RD, separately from this anonymity promise.
        P5Reviewer removal and private access

        Reviewer decisions change public visibility, not creator ownership.

        1. P5-S1 Opens a public Share link after reviewer removal.
        2. P5-S2 The signed-in creator opens their own Result after removal.
        3. P5-S3 Opens the removed item's more menu in History.
        4. P5-S4 Presses the removal notice.

        • Do not mount playable audio or video in unavailable views.

              ✓ Design Decisions
              IDQuestionDecision
              D-01Six reasons only?Keep six built-in reasons plus Something else; always show disabled Submit until valid.
              D-02Success dismissal?Done manually dismisses; no three-second automatic dismissal.
              D-03Authentication and ownership?Guests may report; hide and block self-report after sign-in; official content remains reportable.
              D-04Anti-abuse session?Two controls only: duplicate priority and five mixed-kind successes per document lifetime; refresh resets.
              D-05Toast duration?Three seconds with hover and focus hold, restarting after leave.
              D-06Failure?Keep input, show the approved error, allow retry and do not count failure.
              D-07Removal design and access?Keep the existing notice design; all public access stops, signed-in owner retains History and Result playback.
              D-08QA demonstration?Use the existing QA panel; new failure and reviewer state is session-only and resets on refresh.
